OPTIMIZATION TIPS:
=================
1. FixedPoint: Adjust based on average daily range of your instrument
2. BreakoutFactor: Lower values = more trades, Higher values = fewer but stronger signals
3. VolPeriod: Shorter period = more responsive to recent volatility
4. DeltaResetPeriod: Daily for intraday, Weekly for swing trading
5. MinDeltaThreshold: Set to filter weak delta signals (test with backtest data)

RISK MANAGEMENT:
===============
- Always start with small LotSize (0.01) when testing
- Use proper position sizing (1-2% risk per trade)
- Consider adding stop-loss and take-profit levels
- Backtest thoroughly before live trading
- Monitor Delta Volume indicator for divergences

BACKTESTING RECOMMENDATIONS:
===========================
- Use at least 6-12 months of tick data
- Test on different market conditions (trending, ranging, volatile)
- Pay attention to win rate and average profit per trade
- Check maximum drawdown
- Analyze performance by time of day/day of week
